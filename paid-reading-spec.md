
CozAlyze Paid Reading Spec (final, Aug 24)
Purpose
The paid sidereal reading is one continuous, personal document written to the reader in the second person. It reads like a letter from someone who knows them, not a report. Everything below the surface stays in the engine. Nothing technical is ever visible.
This spec sits in the AI layer of the four-layer profile (Calculation, Data, Presentation, AI). It consumes the full sidereal data set and produces prose against a fixed skeleton.
Reader-facing rules
* Second person throughout. “You,” never “the native” or “the chart holder.”
* Zero technical terminology. No house numbers, no degrees, no nakshatra names, no dasha names, no karaka names, no aspect language, no rulerships, no “sits in the house of,” no sign relationships. “Rising sign,” “north node,” “south node” are allowed.
* Planets appear as influences only. “Jupiter’s influence,” “Venus at its strongest,” “Saturn’s weight.” Never “Mars rules your rising sign,” never “Jupiter is in Mars’s sign.”
* Never explain where a statement comes from. The reader gets the picture, not the mechanics.
* Nakshatra and pada for every body are fully incorporated as motivation, texture, and character, woven into each section, never as a pasted paragraph and never named.
* The whole reading has one voice and one argument. Every section answers to the diagnosis in the first paragraph.
* Both angles in every section: the gift and the cost. Never averaged into a neutral statement.
* Family is never skipped. Mother and father each get a real read even when the chart is thin on one of them.
* No frightening headers. Difficult material is framed as a pull or a current, never as darkness or a flaw.
* Close with one instruction that comes out of the diagnosis. One thing.
* Target length 1,500 to 1,900 words. Ten sections. Short headers.
* No em dashes. Commas only.
Register (ruled Aug 24)
Possibility register throughout, at full depth. The Aug 19 language law applies to the paid reading exactly as to the free copy.
* Chart facts may be stated plainly: that a planet is strong, that an influence sits on the Moon, that a pattern exists.
* Everything about the person’s life, feelings, family, relationships, career, or future is possibility only: “this may show up as,” “there’s a tendency toward,” “your chart suggests,” “this often corresponds with,” “there may have been.”
* Never absolute about a person. Forbidden: “you are,” “you will,” “your father was,” “this will happen,” “you are destined,” any medical or psychological label.
* Vary the softeners so the prose doesn’t chant “may.” Mix may, can, tends to, often, likely, suggests, points toward, there’s a pull toward.
* Depth is not reduced to make room for softeners.
Skeleton
Every reading follows this order. Headers are fixed. Content is generated.
1. The big picture. One paragraph. The single tension the chart turns on. Engine looks for the widest gap: strong planets against weak luminaries, strong lagna against afflicted Moon, big yogas against dusthana weight.
2. Who you are. Lagna, lagna lord, anything on the ascendant, any exchange or yoga fusing identity with creativity, career, or faith. All expressed as influences.
3. Your inner world. Moon sign, house, conjunctions, aspects onto it, waxing or waning, nakshatra and pada, the counterweight named.
4. Home, your mother, your father. 4th house and lord. Moon plus Matrikaraka for the mother. Sun, 9th lord, Pitrikaraka for the father. Both parents always get a paragraph.
5. Love, sex, creativity. 5th and 7th and their lords. Venus and Mars. Darakaraka as the partner portrait.
6. The pull beneath the surface. Nodal axis by house and condition, dispositors, own-star nodes. A push and pull, never a flaw.
7. Work and money. 10th lord and house, 2nd and 11th, Amatyakaraka. Family money versus own output.
8. The threads that run through your life. All seven chara karakas plus the Atmakaraka, one short paragraph each, in this order: core (AK), career path (AmK), allies and siblings (BK), mother and comfort (MK), father and teachers (PiK), creations and children (PK), obstacles (GK), partner (DK). Planet named for the core, the mother, and the father only. Every other thread states the result and nothing else. Karaka names never appear.
9. The timeline. Every mahadasha from birth as a chapter with years and ages. Current antardasha as a stretch. Next two chapters. Current Saturn, Jupiter, and node transits described as influences pressing on areas of life. The chapter the chart seems built for.
10. What I’d want you to take from this. One instruction that resolves section 1.
Engine inputs
Sidereal longitudes to the minute for lagna and nine graha. Nakshatra and pada for every body with pada navamsa. Dignity and condition per graha (exalted, own, friend, enemy, debilitated, retrograde, combust, vargottama). All lordships for the lagna. Graha drishti map. Yogas detected (parivartana, Mahapurusha, Vish, Kemadruma, Budha-Aditya, standard raja and dhana patterns). All seven chara karakas. Vimshottari mahadasha and antardasha with dates, 365.25-day year. Transit positions of Saturn, Jupiter, Rahu, Ketu at read time. Birth time confidence flag.
The reading cannot be templated sentence by sentence and stay personal. It is generated from the full data set with this skeleton as the instruction.
Birth time handling
If the time is unknown or a placeholder, the reading opens with one plain sentence saying so and that the timeline holds regardless. Sections 2 through 8 are written from the Moon chart with ascendant material omitted. The engine never guesses a lagna.
Reference output
Sample chart: Scorpio ascendant sidereal, Libra Moon, Pisces Venus and Mars, Gemini Rahu, Sagittarius Ketu, born into Jupiter, Mercury chapter 2012 to 2028. Birth time was a placeholder. Written to the final rules.

One note before we begin. This reading works from a birth time of midnight. If that’s exact, everything below applies as written. If it’s an estimate, the timeline section holds regardless, and the rest should be read a little more loosely.
The big picture
Your chart is unusual in one specific way. The planets that carry talent, luck, love, and discipline are all unusually strong in you. Jupiter’s influence sits right at the front of your personality, Venus is at its strongest, Saturn is at its strongest, and Mars and Jupiter reinforce each other in a way that tends to fuse a person’s sense of self with their creative life. Meanwhile the Sun and the Moon, the two influences that hold core identity and emotional baseline, are both under some pressure. So there may be a lot of horsepower here and a center that doesn’t always feel steady. Most of what follows comes from that one split. It’s likely you are more capable, more fortunate, and more loved than you feel on an average Tuesday.
Who you are
Jupiter’s influence on how you meet the world often shows up as generosity, expansiveness, and a quick yes, with a natural faith that things will work out. It also tends to tie speech, creativity, and belief directly into identity. You may find there is very little separation between you and what you make. Mars’s influence in your chart runs straight into creativity, romance, and performance, and Mars and Jupiter strengthen each other here, one of the more fortunate patterns a chart can hold. It points the same direction three different ways: what you make may be where you most feel like yourself, and it tends to bring luck with it. This reads as a performer’s chart by architecture, not by mood.
Your inner world
This may be the part of the chart that works against you. Your Moon, the part of you that governs feeling and safety, carries a strong pull toward solitude, loss, dreams, and faraway places, and Saturn’s influence sits right on it. Saturn is strong here, which suggests it isn’t cruel, but it can be heavy. This often shows up as a low, steady undertow: a sense of being on the outside even in a crowd, a need to withdraw, a mood that can go dark without a clear reason, and a pull toward being alone. Your Moon also carries a fierce, goal-fixed quality, the kind that can lock onto something and not let go, which tends to be a gift when it’s pointed at work and a strain when it’s pointed at a person or a fear. Mars’s influence reaches this part of you too, so the heaviness may sometimes turn sudden and sharp. Jupiter is the likely counterweight, and can keep the floor from dropping out. Vivid dreams, an active inner life, and real relief near water or in a foreign place would all be consistent with this Moon. It reads as a Moon that needs solitude on its own terms, and may suffer when isolation happens to it instead.
Home, your mother, your father
The Sun’s influence in your chart is concentrated on home, with Mercury’s alongside it. Home may be where your personality lives and shows. You’d likely put yourself into your space, and your space would tend to look like you. But the same Saturn weight that sits on your Moon also reaches into your home life, while Venus, the planet most connected to your mother, is at its most beautiful and tied to love and creativity. Read together, this suggests a mother who is loved, creative, and central to you, and also some form of distance, whether physical, emotional, or through burdens she may have carried that weren’t yours. The home can be both pride and hiding place at the same time. The signals around your father are fainter and point toward the possibility of absence, distance, or hardness, though how that played out would depend on your circumstances. What’s interesting is that Jupiter, the planet most connected to his lineage, is the strongest planet you have, which suggests that whatever he was or wasn’t, something of the legacy behind him may have landed in you anyway.
Love, sex, creativity
These may be one system in you. Mars and Venus work together in the area of romance and creation, Venus at its strongest, with Jupiter’s blessing over both. Because Venus is tied to both your love life and your partnerships, and Mars to both your creativity and your sense of self, attraction, creativity, sex, and the search for a partner likely run on the same current, and that current tends to run hot. Mars here is usually not loud. It’s more the deep, controlled kind of intensity, the snake under the water, protective of what it makes and who it loves. Venus here tends to be soft, nourishing, and idealizing. Jupiter can make all of it romantic. One possible complication is that Venus’s influence in your chart also reaches into secrecy and retreat, so relationships may carry privacy, foreign elements, and the bedroom as sanctuary. The partner your chart points toward would likely be verbal, rhythmic, musical or witty, younger in spirit, and connected to home. The important ones may arrive through creative circles rather than social ones.
The pull beneath the surface
This is likely an axis you already know about. The south node in your chart is tied to family, money, and speech, and it’s in its purest possible condition. This can suggest coming in with tradition, family, and material stability in your bones, and spending much of life feeling detached from all three. Speech may tend toward blunt, sometimes cutting, usually truthful, the kind that pulls things up by the root. The north node is tied to the hidden, also in its purest condition, and it carries a strong influence over your career path. There may be a deep pull toward what’s underneath: the taboo, the psychological, sex and death and transformation, other people’s secrets and resources, crisis and what comes after it. The Sun and Saturn both feed into this same channel beneath the surface, so identity, career, and home life may all end up routed through the hidden whether or not you plan it that way. This isn’t a flaw. It reads as the subject matter you were built for. The push and pull between the family you came from and the depths you’re drawn to may be a central tension of your life, and it doesn’t usually resolve by picking one.
Work and money
Career and home are tied together in your chart, which often corresponds with work that comes from a home base, or work that is about home, family, interiors, or inner worlds. Your career carries a Leo quality, performance-shaped, with the Sun’s and Mercury’s influence behind it. Performance and speech may be your most natural output. Combined with the north node, this points toward a communicator or performer whose material is the hidden. Money in this chart tends to come through your own voice, your own home, and your own communication rather than through family. Earning power looks good and looks personal. Family money is unlikely to be the source, and may never really have been.
The threads that run through your life
Your core, the part of you that the whole life is organized around, is ruled by the Moon, and it leans toward solitude, harmony, and letting go. The deepest lesson your chart points to may be learning to be at peace in your own company, in faraway places, and in the act of releasing what you can’t hold. Whatever else you build, this is likely where the real work is.
Your career path suggests work that is unconventional, research-driven, and drawn to what’s hidden or transformational. You may be pulled toward fields most people avoid, and you may do your best work where the subject matter is uncomfortable. Sudden turns in your working life would be consistent with this.
You may experience your closest allies, mentors, and siblings as people with a strong, steady identity, connected to your home life. The guidance you trust most may come from figures who are private and a little unconventional rather than public.
Your mother, your comfort, and the way you learn are carried by Venus at its strongest. This points toward a mother who is beautiful, nurturing, and creative, comfort that comes through beauty and art, and learning that happens best through what you make rather than what you’re told.
Your father, your teachers, and the lineage behind you are carried by Jupiter, your strongest planet. Teachers may have mattered enormously to you, and the wisdom you carry may feel like it came from somewhere older than your own life. Faith and philosophy are likely to be a source of strength even if the father himself was distant.
Your creations, and any children, students, or followers, come from passion. What you produce is likely bold and fiercely defended, and you may be protective of it to the point of friction.
Your obstacles tend to arrive as delay, isolation, distance, and slow grinding pressure rather than sudden attack, and much of that pressure may be self-imposed. The same force that creates the obstacle also builds the discipline to outlast it, so your hardest stretches may also be your most formative.
Your partner would likely be verbal, quick, rhythmic or musical, younger in spirit, and someone with whom home is central. The relationship itself may be built as much on conversation as on anything else.
The timeline
Your life runs in long chapters. You were born into a Jupiter chapter, which ended around age ten. Then came a Saturn chapter from about 1993 to 2012, ages ten to twenty nine. With the heavy Moon and Saturn combination running your life during exactly the years you were becoming yourself, those years may have carried moves, a home base that didn’t quite hold, a sense of being on the outside, and a discipline that came out of all of it. Then Mercury from 2012 to late 2028, the chapter you’re in now. This has likely been the home-based, mental, analytical, researching chapter, thinking in circles, holding skills rather than spending them, with that hidden pull under everything. Right now, and through late 2028, you’re in the closing stretch of it, and the closing stretch is colored by Saturn again. Themes of withdrawal, endings, and a pull toward somewhere far away may become more prominent. Saturn’s influence is also currently pressing on your creative and romantic life, and the north node is stirring up home and identity, so the next year and a half may bring pressure on exactly the things you care most about. This could be the hard part before the shift.
In late 2028 the south node chapter begins and runs to 2035. This tends to be an uprooting. Family attachments, money, and possessions may get stripped to what’s real, and your voice may become your instrument. It can feel like loss while functioning as clearing. Then in 2035 Venus takes over for twenty years. Venus is your best planet, working with Mars in the area of love and creation with Jupiter’s blessing over it. That looks like the chapter this whole chart was built for. Whatever you’ve been holding through the Mercury years may finally become usable there.
What I’d want you to take from this
Protect the Moon. Your chart has no shortage of talent, love, or luck. What it may have is an emotional center that keeps suggesting you’re alone in a way the rest of your chart doesn’t support. Solitude you choose can feed you. Isolation you drift into can starve you. If you learn the difference, the next two years may get a lot easier.
